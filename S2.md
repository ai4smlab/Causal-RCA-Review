## 📎 Supplementary Material 📋 S2. Studies Excluded After Full-Text Assessment and Reasons for Exclusion

During title and abstract screening, 723 records were excluded because they clearly fell outside the scope of software-defined systems, formal causal root cause analysis, or both. Consistent with PRISMA recommendations, detailed exclusion tracking was maintained at the full-text screening stage. Consequently, this supplementary material reports the 47 studies excluded after full-text assessment together with their corresponding primary exclusion reasons.
