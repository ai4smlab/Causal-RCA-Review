## 📎 Supplementary Material 📋 S2. Full-Text Excluded Studies and Exclusion Rationale

During title and abstract screening, 723 records were excluded because they clearly fell outside the scope of distributed software-intensive systems, formal causal root cause analysis, or both. Consistent with PRISMA recommendations, detailed exclusion tracking was maintained at the full-text screening stage. Consequently, this supplementary section reports the 52 studies excluded after full-text assessment together with the corresponding exclusion rationale.
